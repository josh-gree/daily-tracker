    Introduction

    This daily activity tracker helps you monitor your activity load, nutrition, movement, sleep, and energy levels. Over 7–14 days, track your daily routine to establish your baseline and identify what works best for you. We recommend logging consistently for 7–14 days to effectively gauge your progress.

    Use the energy scale to rate your morning and evening energy levels on a scale of 1 to 10. Record your meals and water intake, noting how you feel after each meal. Document your daily activities, including walks, chores, work, or school hours. Reflect on your day with notes on what worked well, what didn't, and areas to focus on tomorrow.

    By staying mindful of your activities, energy levels, and well-being, you can make meaningful strides towards your health goals.
